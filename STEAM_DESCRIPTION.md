[h1]Mod Information[/h1]
Geb's Animals adds [b]15 new animals[/b] to DayZ: chimpanzee, alien, penguin, rabbit, camel, elephant, giraffe, male and female turkey, peacock, black bear, polar bear, panda bear, raccoon and cheetah. Each one has its own model, sounds and harvest.

It's meant to make the world feel more alive, not to hand out free food: the animals are Central Economy encounters, and the server owner decides which species appear, where they live and how common they are.

[b]Website (every animal, the setup guide and all the classnames):[/b] [url=https://packjc.github.io/gebsanimals/]packjc.github.io/gebsanimals[/url]

[h1]Requirements[/h1]
[list]
[*] DayZ 1.30.
[*] No other mods. Load it on both the server and every client.
[/list]

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]15 wildlife species[/b], from rabbits and turkeys to bears, camels, giraffes and elephants, and one unusual visitor: a green alien.
[*] [b]A harvest for each animal:[/b] its own meat, pelts, guts, bones and lard, a raccoon's ringed tail and an elephant's tusks.
[*] [b]Crafting:[/b] prepare a turkey with a knife, saw, machete, hatchet or other blade, and craft a coonskin cap from a raccoon pelt and tail.
[*] [b]Server-controlled by design:[/b] nothing replaces vanilla wildlife. You add only the species you want, through your own Central Economy events and territories.
[/list]

[h1]Server Setup[/h1]
Animals don't go in types.xml. For each species you want:
[list]
[*] Copy one of your map's vanilla animal events in [b]db/events.xml[/b], give it a new name, and swap in a [b]geb_[/b] animal.
[*] Add a matching block in [b]cfgeventspawns.xml[/b] with territory positions from your own map.
[/list]
Start with one species and add more gradually: too many animal events can crowd out vanilla wildlife. The website has copy-ready XML and a troubleshooting FAQ.

[h1]Coming from Geb's Flora & Fauna[/h1]
This mod used to be Geb's Flora & Fauna. It is now three mods, so servers can run the parts they want: [b]Geb's Animals[/b] (this one), [b]Geb's Plants[/b] (garden crops and wild ginseng) and [b]Geb's Mushrooms[/b] (63 wild fungi). The classnames haven't changed. If your server ran Geb's Flora & Fauna, load all three to keep the items players already have.
