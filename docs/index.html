GFF / Field Guide

A wider wilderness

More life between the towns.

Geb's Flora & Fauna broadens the survival sandbox without prescribing one universal ecosystem. Server owners choose the species, territories, and population limits that fit their map.

01

Wildlife

Fifteen creatures range from rabbits and turkeys to elephants, polar bears, and stranger encounters.

02

Harvesting

Animals provide their own meats and, where configured, pelts, tusks, tails, guts, bones, and lard.

03

Mycology

44 fungi to forage: chanterelles, morels, boletes, the sky-blue indigo milk cap and more, beside deadly look-alikes like the death cap, the funeral bell and the jack-o'-lantern.

04

Fieldcraft

Prepare harvested turkey and turn a raccoon pelt plus tail into a wearable skin cap.

05

Gardening

Grow corn, okra, green beans, onions, garlic, yellow squash, watermelon, cantaloupe, carrots and cucumbers from seed packets in a garden plot, then cook the harvest, slice the melons or cut fresh seeds back out of it.

Server-controlled by design. Nothing has to replace vanilla wildlife. Add only the event definitions and spawn positions you want.

Server-owner guide

Deploy the ecosystem.

The process follows DayZ's normal Central Economy animal-event pattern: define a type in events.xml, give it territories in cfgeventspawns.xml, and let the CE maintain the population.

  1. 01

    Install the Workshop mod

    Place @gebsflorafauna beside the server executable, copy its key into keys/, and load -mod=@gebsflorafauna on the server and clients.

  2. 02

    Choose an animal classname

    Start with one species while testing—for example geb_Racoon. Animal classes do not belong in types.xml; dynamic animal populations are managed through events.

  3. 03

    Add an event definition

    Copy an existing vanilla animal event in db/events.xml, give it a unique event name, and replace its child type with the desired geb_ classname. Keep the same structure and tune nominal/min/max for your server.

    <event name="AnimalGebRacoon">
    <nominal>8</nominal>
    <min>4</min>
    <max>12</max>
    <lifetime>180</lifetime>
    <restock>0</restock>
    <saferadius>200</saferadius>
    <distanceradius>50</distanceradius>
    <cleanupradius>200</cleanupradius>
    <flags deletable="0" init_random="0" remove_damaged="1"/>
    <position>fixed</position>
    <limit>child</limit>
    <active>1</active>
    <children>
        <child lootmax="0" lootmin="0" max="4" min="1" type="geb_Racoon"/>
    </children>
</event>
  4. 04

    Add map territories

    In cfgeventspawns.xml, add a matching event block inside <eventposdef>. Replace the example coordinates with safe locations from your map. Each <pos> is a possible territory center; the zone values control group spread.

    <event name="AnimalGebRacoon">
    <zone smin="0" smax="0" dmin="40" dmax="80" r="100"/>
    <!-- Replace these with coordinates for your map. -->
    <pos x="7500.0" z="7500.0" a="0.0"/>
    <pos x="8000.0" z="7200.0" a="0.0"/>
</event>
  5. 05

    Add forest fungi (optional)

    Fungi are loot, not animal events. Give each one a types.xml entry like vanilla's AgaricusMushroom (nominal 0, lifetime 900, category food), then add it as a child of the vanilla Trajectory events in events.xml, which drop mushrooms on the forest floor around players. Leave out the poisonous species if your server should not have them.

    <type name="geb_Chanterelle">
    <nominal>0</nominal>
    <lifetime>900</lifetime>
    <restock>0</restock>
    <min>0</min>
    <quantmin>-1</quantmin>
    <quantmax>-1</quantmax>
    <cost>100</cost>
    <flags count_in_cargo="0" count_in_hoarder="0" count_in_map="1" count_in_player="0" crafted="1" deloot="0"/>
    <category name="food"/>
</type>

  7. 07

    Validate and test

    Stop the server before editing, keep backups, validate the XML, restart, and inspect the RPT and script logs. Add species one at a time. Excessive totals across all animal events can suppress or destabilize other wildlife populations.

Use the current map's own vanilla animal events as your template.CE values that feel reasonable on Chernarus may not suit Sakhal, Livonia, or a custom terrain.

Troubleshooting

Common questions.

Does this replace vanilla animals?+

No. Custom events can coexist with vanilla wildlife. Your mission configuration decides which events are active.

Why can I spawn an animal manually but not find it naturally?+

A working classname confirms the mod loaded, but natural populations still require matching entries in events.xml and cfgeventspawns.xml.

Why did vanilla animals stop spawning?+

Check the server logs for malformed XML and review the combined nominal/min/max totals of every animal event. Start with one custom event and add more gradually.

Which mushrooms are poisonous?+

Death Cap, Destroying Angel, False Morel, Jack-o'-Lantern, Common Earthball, Sulphur Tuft, Deadly Webcap, Green-spored Parasol, Panther Cap, Funeral Bell, The Sickener, Yellow Stainer, Ivory Funnel, Brown Roll-rim, Devil's Bolete, Deadly Dapperling, Deadly Fibrecap, Livid Pinkgill, False Death Cap and Early False Morel. Most cause food poisoning (vomiting; charcoal tablets help). The Death Cap, Destroying Angel, Funeral Bell, Deadly Dapperling and Deadly Webcap are deadly: their toxins survive cooking and cause organ damage that only chelation tablets stop. The Brown Roll-rim builds up the same damage over several meals. The Deadly Fibrecap and Ivory Funnel strike within the hour: drenching sweat, tears and a failing pulse. The False Morel is dangerous raw or cooked, and only partly safer boiled. Morels, Wood Blewit, Wine Cap, Bay Boletes and Chicken of the Woods upset the stomach raw, and Honey Mushrooms and Orange Birch Boletes must be cooked.

How do the garden crops grow?+

Empty a Geb's Garden seed packet, then plant the seeds in a tilled garden plot and water them, as with vanilla seeds. Each crop grows through five stages; harvest it when ripe, before it dries or spoils. Cut seeds out of a raw crop with any blade (garlic splits into cloves; carrots have none, so keep a packet). A whole watermelon or cantaloupe is too big to eat: prepare it with a blade for slices and seeds, as with vanilla's pumpkin. Raw green beans upset the stomach; cook them.

Does every player need the mod?

Yes. It supplies custom models, textures, sounds, scripts, and localized item data.

Can I put it in a server pack?

No. Use the authorized Workshop item and a Steam Collection for your server's required mods.
